1 · What is Gravitation?

Gravitation = universal attractive force between every pair of masses.

Galileo: with no air, all bodies fall with the same acceleration g — watch!

Newton: the apple 🍎, the Moon 🌙, tides and planets obey ONE law.

2 · Models of the Solar System

Ptolemy: everything circles the Earth → Aryabhatta & Copernicus: planets circle the Sun.

Tycho Brahe's data → Kepler's 3 laws → Newton's universal law.

3 · Kepler's 1st Law – Orbits

Planets move in ellipses with the Sun at one focus.

Ellipse: PF₁ + PF₂ = constant (pins + string + pencil!). Circle = special case.

P = perihelion (nearest, fastest) · A = aphelion (farthest, slowest)

4 · Kepler's 2nd Law – Areas

Line Sun→planet sweeps equal areas in equal times (orange patch: shape changes, area same).

ΔA/Δt = L/2m = constant → angular momentum conserved.

m rPvP = m rAvA ⇒ vP/vA = rA/rP

Trap: holds for ANY central force, not only 1/r².

5 · Kepler's 3rd Law – Periods

T² ∝ a³ — far planets are much slower.

T² = (4π²/GM) a³ · same constant for all planets of one star

T²/a³ (×10⁻³⁴): Earth 2.96 · Mars 2.98 · Jupiter 3.01 ✔

Mars year = (1.52)^{3/2} × 365 ≈ 684 days · Elliptical orbit: use a.

6 · Newton's Universal Law

F = G m₁m₂ / r²

Attractive · central · F₁₂ = −F₂₁ · independent of medium.

Double the distance → force becomes ¼.

Moon test: g/am = (Rm/RE)² ≈ 3600 → inverse-square confirmed! G = 6.67×10⁻¹¹ N m²/kg² [M⁻¹L³T⁻²]

7 · Superposition Principle

Net force = vector sum: FR = F₁ + F₂ + … + Fn

3 equal forces, 120° apart (Ex 7.2) → FR = 0

Double the mass at A → 4Gm² up, 2Gm² + 2Gm² cancel partly → net = 2Gm² towards A

8 · Shell Theorem

Outside: acts as if all mass is at the centre.

Inside a shell: F = 0 (no gravitational shielding!).

Solid uniform sphere: inside g ∝ r · outside g ∝ 1/r²

9 · Cavendish – Finding G

Big balls pull small ones → torque → fine wire twists by θ.

G(Mm/d²)·L = τθ → measure θ → get G.

“Cavendish weighed the Earth”: ME = gR²/G ≈ 5.97×10²⁴ kg

10 · Acceleration due to Gravity g

g = GM/R² ≈ 9.8 m/s² (m cancels — independent of the body's mass)

g = (4/3)πGρR · depends on planet's M and R only

Moon: g ≈ g/6, vₑ = 2.3 km/s → no atmosphere

11 · g at a Height h

g(h) = GM/(R+h)² = g(1+h/R)⁻²

h ≪ R: g(h) ≈ g(1 − 2h/R)

Ex 7.15: 63 N at surface → at h = R/2: 63 × 4/9 = 28 N

12 · g Inside the Earth

Only the inner sphere pulls: g(d) = g(1 − d/R) (uniform density)

Centre: g = 0 · Surface: g is maximum

Ex 7.16: 250 N at surface → half-way down = 125 N

13 · Gravitational Potential Energy

U = −GMm/r (zero at ∞) · potential V = −GM/r

ΔU = GMm(1/r₁ − 1/r₂) ≈ mgh only when h ≪ R

System: add over all pairs. Square of side l: U = −(4 + 2/√2) Gm²/l = −5.41 Gm²/l

14 · Escape Speed

Escape: total energy ≥ 0 → ½mv² = GMm/R

vₑ = √(2GM/R) = √(2gR) = 11.2 km/s

Independent of mass & direction · vₑ = √2 × v_orbit (7.9 km/s)

Thrown at 3vₑ → speed far away = √8 vₑ ≈ 31.7 km/s

15 · Earth Satellites

Gravity = centripetal force: mv²/(R+h) = GMm/(R+h)²

v = √(GM/(R+h)) · near surface v = √(gR) = 7.9 km/s

T = 2π√((R+h)³/GM) → T² ∝ (R+h)³ · near surface T ≈ 85 min

Higher orbit → slower, longer period. Weightlessness = free fall, not zero g!

16 · Energy of a Satellite

K = GMm/2r (+) · U = −GMm/r · E = −K = U/2

E < 0 → bound orbit · binding energy = +GMm/2r

Ex 7.8: 400 kg, 2R → 4R needs ΔE = mgR/8 = 3.13×10⁹ J

17 · Fun Activities 🎉

🥤Bottle with a hole, filled with water: drop it — leaking stops (free fall)!

18 · JEE / NEET Traps

g(h) ≈ g(1−2h/R) only for h ≪ R

g(d) = g(1−d/R): linear inside

vₑ: no mass, no direction dependence

Satellite: E = −K, U = 2E

Bigger r → smaller v, bigger T

Inside shell F = 0; outside → point mass

19 · Quick Quiz

Q1. Weight 63 N on surface. Weight at h = R/2?

63 × (R/1.5R)² = 63 × 4/9 = 28 N

Q2. 250 N on surface. Weight half-way to the centre?

g(d) = g(1 − ½) → 125 N

Q3. Planet's year is half Earth's. Orbit size?

T² ∝ r³ → r = (½)^{2/3} = 0.63 rE
